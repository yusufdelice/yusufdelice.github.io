About Me

I am a Computer Engineer based in Istanbul, specializing in backend architecture, enterprise data pipelines, and intelligent automation. I graduated from Istanbul Commerce University and have since built a career bridging the gap between massive corporate infrastructures and modern, scalable web technologies.

My engineering background is rooted in managing and developing end-to-end ERP ecosystems for global manufacturing operations. I am accustomed to working with high-volume databases, writing complex SQL optimizations, and ensuring transaction security for critical business modules. Beyond corporate integrations, I co-founded Yuta Information Technologies, an agency where I architect full-stack web solutions, deploy autonomous AI agents for real-world applications, and manage independent VPS infrastructures.

Whether I am reverse-engineering legacy systems, writing data extraction pipelines, or configuring Docker deployments, I focus on building modular, secure, and performant solutions that solve tangible business problems.
